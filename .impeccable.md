## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, design peers, and collaborators reviewing Liao's product-design experience. They need to understand project scope, process, craft, and personality quickly without losing access to detailed case studies.

### Brand Personality
Playful, personal, and precise. The home page should feel like a memorable retro desktop with small pixel-art surprises, while project content remains professional, calm, and easy to evaluate.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light-blue desktop scene combines tactile photographed objects, restrained pixel art, and macOS-inspired windows. Motion should feel physical and responsive rather than decorative. Avoid generic SaaS cards, excessive glass effects, neon gradients, and exaggerated bounces.

### Design Principles
1. Preserve the authored desktop-world metaphor and make every interaction behave like a physical object.
2. Use one clear motion idea per element; keep feedback subtle, fast, and interruptible.
3. Keep portfolio information legible and left aligned even when the surrounding presentation is playful.
4. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid animation that blocks navigation.
5. Prefer distinctive supplied assets and pixel details over generic icons or generated decoration.
